How Amazon Management Works

The process is simple, and you stay in control of the money. We do the work; you approve every purchase. Here's how a store goes from zero to selling.

1

Set Up Your Marketplace Account

You create your Amazon seller account (we send the exact steps). It stays in your name, with your LLC, EIN, bank, and tax info. You always own it. Already selling? We take over from where you are.

2

Connect It to Ecom Circles

You link your store to Ecom Circles and add our team as a user. We’re an approved Amazon vendor, so we operate through the connection and never log into your raw account.

3

Fund Your Inventory

You add funds inside Ecom Circles (most clients start with $5,000 to $10,000). Your balance and every dollar spent are visible in your dashboard.

4

We Find What Your Account Can Sell

We run thousands of brands and fresh supplier lists against your account to map exactly what you’re approved to sell. New accounts start gated, so we find the openings.

5

We Build Your Purchase Order

We match approved brands to real, in-stock products from our wholesale network and send you the buy list to review. Nothing is bought until you confirm it.

6

We Buy, Receive & List

On your approval we place the PO, our warehouse checks in every unit, and our team lists them to your store. You watch the whole PO and check-in inside Ecom Circles.

7

We Run & Grow the Store

We price competitively, handle customer service and returns, manage platform issues, and double down on what sells. You track sales and profit in one place. Profits split 50/50.

Who Should Use Amazon Management?

This is right for people who want to own an Amazon business without running it day to day. Not everyone is a fit, and we'll tell you honestly if you're not.

Good Fit

  • Want a done-for-you Amazon store without running it yourself
  • Have capital for the setup fee plus inventory ($5K-$10K to start)
  • Comfortable funding inventory and letting us make the day-to-day calls
  • Want owned inventory (not dropshipping) so the store stays compliant
  • In it for the long game as profit compounds through inventory

Not Ideal Fit

  • Want to run and control the account yourself day to day (DIY fits better)
  • Can't fund inventory beyond the setup fee

DIY gives you full control but eats your time: sourcing, buying, listing, repricing, and customer service can run 40+ hours a week. A freelancer is cheaper ($1,500 to $3,000 a month) but usually juggles several clients and won't source or buy inventory for you. Automation companies do run the whole store like we do, and some hold inventory in their own warehouse, but they often charge $25,000 to $100,000 up front, run on someone else's tools, and give you little visibility into what's happening day to day. Ecom Circles pairs that full-service model with our own software: you watch every purchase order, buy, and check-in in real time, we cap our roster so your store gets real attention, and because we take 50/50 of profit, we only make money when you do.
