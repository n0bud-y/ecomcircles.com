What Makes an Account Valuable

These factors determine your account’s market value

Account Age

Older accounts with established selling history are more valuable. We look for accounts that are at least 6 months old.

Feedback & Ratings

Positive seller feedback and strong performance metrics increase account value significantly.

Sales History

Consistent sales volume and revenue history demonstrate a proven, active store.

Account Health

Clean account standing with no active policy violations, suspensions, or unresolved performance issues.

Category Approvals

Ungated categories (especially restricted ones like grocery, beauty, or health) increase value substantially.

Brand Registry

Brand-registered accounts with trademark protections command premium pricing.

From inquiry to payment in five straightforward steps

Submit Your Account Details
