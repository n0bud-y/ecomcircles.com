How It Works

Without Returns Management: Returns go straight to liquidation bins at a fraction of their value.

With Returns Management: Each return is inspected individually. Resellable items get cleaned, tested, and relisted. The rest is liquidated intelligently.

The result: You recover value from returns that would otherwise be written off. $3 flat rate per return, no percentage of recovery.

Returns Management in Your Workflow

Returns management works best when connected to your order fulfillment. Manage all your orders in one dashboard while we handle returns intake, inspection, and refurbishment in the background. As items are relisted, they sync back to your inventory.

For sellers using our complete warehouse services, returns management integrates seamlessly with your inbound stock, fulfillment, and inventory tracking. When a customer return arrives at our facility, it goes through our inspection and refurbishment process automatically, keeping your inventory levels accurate in real time.
