What is WFS Prep?

WFS (Walmart Fulfillment Services) is Walmart's equivalent to Amazon FBA. When you send products to WFS, Walmart handles the storage, picking, packing, and shipping to customers. WFS prep means we get your inventory Walmart-ready: inspected, labeled with exact specifications, and packed in the format Walmart requires.

Instead of spending time figuring out Walmart's complex labeling requirements, managing your own prep team, or dealing with rejected shipments, you send your inventory to Ecom Circles. We handle all the technical details. You focus on selling.
