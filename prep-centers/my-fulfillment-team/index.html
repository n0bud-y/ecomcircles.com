My Fulfillment Team

Dresden, TN, USA
No minimumApparel
Visit website

Few prep centers can claim the longevity My Fulfillment Team does, having been one of Amazon's first FBA prep warehouses and reporting more than 10 million items fulfilled to date. The operation works out of a 100,000 square foot facility in Dresden, Tennessee, far larger than most independent preps, and runs its own in-house software platform rather than a third-party WMS. Pricing is a la carte: standard receive, inspect, and label runs $1.45 per unit, dropping to $1.15 at 30 units and up, with a $10 minimum processing fee per order that ranks among the lowest in the industry. There is a one-time $189 ecommerce setup fee and storage at $0.70 per cubic foot monthly with no long-term penalties. Same-day weekday shipping is offered on top of the standard 24 to 48 hour turnaround. It also handles apparel, books and media, and fragile goods.

What sets them apart

  • Claims to be one of Amazon's first FBA prep warehouses; reports experience fulfilling over 10 million items
  • 100,000 sq ft warehouse in Dresden, TN - significantly larger than most independent prep centers
  • Operates proprietary in-house software platform (app.mftlogin.com) - not a third-party WMS
  • Offers same-day shipping on weekdays in addition to standard 24 - 48 hour turnaround; $10 order minimum is among the lowest in the industry
