Leeline Sourcing

China
TikTok Shop
Visit website

Leeline Sourcing combines China sourcing with FBA prep across four warehouse cities, Guangzhou, Shenzhen, Yiwu, and Ningbo, which puts prep close to where the goods are made. The company points to 10 years of sourcing experience, 6,000+ clients served, 30,000+ products sourced, and 150+ million-dollar sellers among its accounts. One detail stands out for sellers tired of opaque China-agent quotes: Leeline publishes itemized per-service pricing on its site, which is uncommon for the category. Inspection runs $0.10 per item, FNSKU labeling and polybagging $0.20, and bundling $0.30, each with a $20 minimum per order. Beyond prep, it runs a proprietary dropshipping app at app.ileeline.com with Shopify, WooCommerce, and Magento integration, and covers customs clearance, photography, and storage with 14 days free. Turnaround is quoted at 48 to 72 hours. The mix suits private label, wholesale, import, and 2-step dropshipping sellers sourcing from China for Amazon, TikTok Shop, Shopify, and eBay.

What sets them apart

  • Claims 6,000+ clients served and 30,000+ products sourced with 10 years of sourcing experience
  • Publishes itemized per-service pricing on website - unusual transparency for a China sourcing agent
  • Operates a proprietary dropshipping app (app.ileeline.com) with Shopify/WooCommerce/Magento integration
  • Claims to have served 150+ million-dollar sellers
