Above All Prep and Ship

Montana, USA5.0(24)
Walmart WFSTax-free MTHazmat
Visit website

Above All Prep and Ship operates from tax-free Montana, where inventory moving through the warehouse picks up no sales tax, a point the team leads with on its homepage. Its published per-unit rates are among the more granular in the directory, sliding from $1.25 for 1 to 50 units a week down to $0.40 once weekly volume tops 10,000. That deep volume curve fits sellers scaling into wholesale and private label, while a $10 surcharge on shipments under 35 items signals it leans toward larger consignments. The service list is broad: hazmat at $5.00 per SKU per shipment, fragile handling at $3.50, FBM and DTC fulfillment at a flat $3.00 per unit, and WFS FedEx pickups at $8.74 each. Worth reading the fine print: a $50 one-time setup fee applies, and items in storage past three months become company property. Google reviewers rate it 5.0 across 24 reviews.

What sets them apart

  • Operates in tax-free Montana, meaning no sales tax on inventory passing through the warehouse - an explicit selling point stated on the homepage.
  • Published tiered per-unit pricing drops from $1.25 (1 - 50 units/week) all the way to $0.40 (10,000+ units/week), with a $50 one-time setup fee and a $10 surcharge for shipments under 35 items.
  • Charges a $5.00 per-SKU-per-shipment fee for HazMat/restricted goods and $8.74 per WFS FedEx pickup, indicating active Walmart WFS outbound logistics capability.
  • Storage is free for the first 30 days then $20/month per line item, with a policy that items stored 3+ months become company property.
  • Offers FBM/DTC fulfillment at a flat $3.00/unit and fragile-item handling at $3.50/unit, plus emergency order processing for a $30 surcharge.
