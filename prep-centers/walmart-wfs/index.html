Almost every prep center on the market will handle Amazon FBA. How Walmart WFS prep differs from Amazon FBA prep

WFS and FBA share the same core handling: receiving, inspection, labeling, polybagging, suffocation labels, sticker removal, and box content labels. What changes is the spec the inventory is prepped to. Walmart Fulfillment Services has its own inbound and labeling requirements, so a center that is fluent in Amazon's spec is not automatically set up for Walmart's. That is the single biggest reason a Walmart seller should filter for WFS support rather than assume it. It is also worth separating two things people conflate: WFS prep means a center preps and ships your stock into Walmart's fulfillment network, which is different from you fulfilling Walmart Marketplace orders yourself. This facet covers WFS-inbound prep. A center that names Walmart WFS as a service, like Chronos Prep Center in Dallas, TX, is telling you it has done the work to meet that spec.

Frequently asked questions

How is Walmart WFS prep different from Amazon FBA prep?

The hands-on work is similar (receiving, inspection, labeling, polybagging, and box content labels), but the inventory is prepped to a different spec. Walmart Fulfillment Services has its own inbound and labeling requirements, separate from Amazon's FBA rules. A center that handles FBA well is not automatically set up for WFS, which is why this directory treats WFS support as its own filter.

Why do fewer prep centers offer Walmart WFS than Amazon FBA?

Most prep centers grew up serving Amazon sellers first, and FBA is close to table stakes in the industry. Meeting Walmart's distinct WFS inbound spec is extra work that not every shop has invested in, so WFS-capable centers are a smaller pool. In our directory, 36 centers are confirmed to prep for WFS, which is why filtering for it saves you from assuming support that may not be there.

Does a WFS prep center handle Walmart's inbound labeling and box content requirements?

A center that genuinely supports WFS preps your inventory to Walmart's inbound and labeling spec, which includes the box and carton content labeling Walmart requires on the way in. Because the requirements differ from Amazon's, confirm WFS specifically rather than relying on a generic 'we do prep' claim. Centers that name Walmart WFS as a stated service, such as Chronos Prep Center in Dallas, have signaled they meet that spec.

Is WFS prep the same as fulfilling Walmart Marketplace orders myself?

No. WFS prep means a center preps and ships your stock into Walmart Fulfillment Services, and Walmart then stores, picks, packs, and ships orders to customers. Fulfilling Walmart Marketplace orders yourself (seller-fulfilled) means you or your 3PL ship each order direct. This page covers WFS-inbound prep, which is the distinct service most Walmart sellers using WFS are looking for.

Can one prep center do both Walmart WFS and Amazon FBA from the same inventory?

Yes, many of the centers here do both, which lets you split a single received shipment between Walmart and Amazon. Chronos Prep Center, for example, lists Walmart WFS prep alongside Amazon FBA, and several others in the list support multiple channels. If you sell on both marketplaces, filtering for WFS and confirming FBA on the center's profile lets you keep prep under one roof.
