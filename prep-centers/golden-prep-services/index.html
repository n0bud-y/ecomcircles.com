Golden Prep Services

Fredericktown, MO5.0(1)
Visit website

Golden Prep Services operates from a small Madison County town, with its warehouse at 1000 E Main St Suite B in Fredericktown, Missouri. The contact details hint at distributed ownership: the site lists both a local 573 number and a New York 929 number, and the business is unusually active on TikTok at @goldenprepservices alongside Facebook, Instagram, and LinkedIn, an uncommon social mix for a rural prep center. It serves Amazon FBA and merchant-fulfilled sellers working online arbitrage, wholesale, and private label, with core services spanning receiving, inspection, FNSKU labeling, polybagging, box content labels, and sticker removal. Pallet storage is available in both short-term and long-term configurations. The brand markets transparent pricing and no surprise fees, but it publishes no rate card, so all pricing is by custom quote. Sellers should request a written quote up front.
