How Walmart Account Management Works

Your Walmart success follows a repeatable system: we audit your entire account, build a growth strategy, then execute daily across listings, pricing, and advertising. You receive weekly reports and have direct access to your account manager for strategy questions.

1

Set Up Your Walmart Account

You create your Walmart seller account (we send the steps). It stays in your name, with your business, bank, and tax info. Walmart is selective, so we help you put the strongest application forward.

2

Connect It to Ecom Circles

You link your store to Ecom Circles and add our team. We run everything through the connection and our software, so support calls and verifications come to us, not you.

3

Fund Your Inventory

You add funds inside Ecom Circles (most clients start with $5,000 to $10,000). Your balance and every dollar spent are visible in your dashboard.

4

We Find What Your Account Can Sell

We run thousands of brands and fresh supplier lists against your account to map what you’re approved to sell. Walmart opens more categories on day one than Amazon does.

5

We Build Your Purchase Order

We match approved brands to real, in-stock products from our wholesale network and send you the buy list. Nothing is bought until you confirm it.

6

We Buy, Receive & List

On your approval we place the PO, our warehouse checks in every unit, and our team lists them. We fulfill through WFS or the warehouse, whichever is more profitable per product.

7

We Run & Grow the Store

We price competitively, handle customer service and returns, watch your seller metrics, and double down on what sells. You track sales and profit in one place. Profits split 50/50.

You stay in control. We're your execution partner. Every Friday, you get a growth report and have a standing check-in call with your account manager.
