How to Sell on Walmart Marketplace: Complete Guide for Sellers

The definitive roadmap to getting approved, setting up your account, choosing fulfillment, managing pricing, and scaling to $50K+ in monthly sales on Walmart.

Walmart Marketplace represents one of the most underrated opportunities in ecommerce. While Amazon dominates industry conversation, Walmart has quietly become the second-largest online marketplace in North America, with 240 million monthly visitors, significantly lower competition per listing, and a growing emphasis on quality resellers and wholesalers. The 2026 landscape has fundamentally shifted: stricter fraud enforcement, lower WFS fulfillment costs for new sellers, and platform consolidation mean that approved sellers now have unprecedented advantages.

Getting approved remains the single biggest barrier. Approval rates hover around 15-25% for first-time applicants, but sellers who understand Walmart's approval criteria and documentation requirements dramatically improve their chances. Once approved, scaling becomes significantly easier than on Amazon: fewer competitors, lower fees, and powerful automation tools level the playing field.

This comprehensive guide covers every step: eligibility requirements, the approval process, account setup, product listing strategy, fulfillment options, pricing management, and advanced scaling tactics. Quick Eligibility Checklist

Before applying, ensure you meet all of these requirements. Missing even one typically results in denial or delayed approval.

Registered US business entity

Proven ecommerce experience

Minimum 1 year selling on Amazon, eBay, Shopify, or another marketplace

Valid business documentation

Tax ID letter, business license, W-9 or equivalent (ready to upload during approval)

GS1-verified barcodes for most products. Walmart validates these before approval

1-2 day fulfillment capability

Either seller-fulfilled with reliable 2-day delivery, or WFS enrollment

Clean seller history

No suspensions, account closures, or fraud flags on other platforms

US-based bank account

For payouts via Payoneer or Hyperwallet

Pro tip: If you don't have 1+ years of ecommerce history, build it first. Make 10+ sales on Amazon or eBay, maintain 95%+ positive feedback, and wait 3-6 months. This significantly improves your Walmart approval chances.

The Approval Process Step-by-Step

1Apply at marketplace.walmart.com

Visit marketplace.walmart.com and click "Request to Sell." The application asks for: business name, business structure, US address, Tax ID, phone number, product categories, estimated annual sales, your ecommerce experience, and fulfillment method preference.

Timeline: 5-10 minutes to complete. Have your business documents and product catalog list ready.

2Submit Documentation for Verification

After initial submission, Walmart requests documentation. Typical requirements:

  • • Tax ID letter (from IRS)
  • • Business license or formation documents
  • • W-9 or equivalent tax form
  • • Product catalog (spreadsheet with SKU, GTIN, product names)
  • • Ecommerce experience documentation (seller dashboard screenshots, performance metrics)

Timeline: 1-3 days. Respond within 48 hours of receiving the request, as delays can push approval back weeks.

3Walmart's Verification Phase

Walmart verifies your documentation, checks GTIN validity, and validates your ecommerce history. They may check competitor listings (if you're entering a restricted category like beauty or supplements), verify tax ID authenticity, and review your seller history on other platforms.

Timeline: 3-5 days for initial verification. Walmart may request additional documentation if anything doesn't match.

4Approval Decision

Walmart issues an approval or denial decision. Approved sellers receive an email with next steps. Denied sellers receive brief feedback (usually "does not meet our current seller requirements") with option to request more details or reapply after 30 days.

Timeline: 2-4 weeks from initial application. Fast-track approvals (5-7 days) occasionally happen if documentation is pristine.

5Complete Seller Center Registration

Once approved, create your Seller Center account and complete setup: accept Retailer Agreement, configure tax forms, connect payment method (Payoneer or Hyperwallet), set up shipping templates, and define return policy.

Timeline: 1 week to fully configure.

Common Denial Reasons & How to Appeal

  • Documentation mismatch (42% of denials): Reapply with corrected documents matching your business registration exactly.
  • Weak ecommerce history: Build 50+ sales on another platform, maintain 95%+ feedback, and reapply after 6 months.
  • Restricted category issues: If selling beauty/supplements, provide COA (Certificate of Analysis) proving product authenticity.
  • GTIN validation failure: Verify all GTINs are correct and GS1-registered before reapplying.

Account Setup in Seller Center

Once you receive approval, Seller Center setup takes about 1 week. Here's the checklist:

Seller Profile Configuration

Add your business name, seller logo, customer service phone, return address, and company description. This information appears on your public seller storefront.

Payment & Tax Setup

Connect a US bank account via Payoneer or Hyperwallet. Submit your W-9 or equivalent tax form. Walmart pays out weekly (if you meet minimum thresholds). Expect 2-3 weeks for first payout processing.

Shipping Configuration

Set up shipping templates: define shipping methods (standard, expedited, 2-day), rates by weight/zone, handling time (1-2 days), and return policies. Walmart defaults to 3-5 day shipping but incentivizes 2-day sellers with Buy Box preference.

Return & Refund Policy

Walmart requires clear return policies. Minimum: 30-day returns accepted. Pro sellers typically offer 30-60 days. Define refund processing time (standard is 5-7 business days after return received).

Team Members & Permissions

Invite team members with specific roles: admin (full access), content manager (edit listings), customer service (handle support), operations (manage orders/shipments). Walmart supports multi-user accounts with role-based permissions.

Setup tip: Don't activate listings until all configurations are complete. Test with 5-10 products first, verify they appear correctly, monitor order flow, and debug any shipping/payment issues before scaling to hundreds of SKUs.

Product Listing Strategy: Getting Seen & Converting

Walmart's search algorithm prioritizes listing quality and seller performance. Unlike Amazon where keyword stuffing can work, Walmart rewards clear, scannable product data.

GTIN/UPC Requirements

Walmart requires valid GS1-verified GTIN/UPC codes for 99% of products. GTINs link your listing to manufacturer data. images, category placement, reviews. Without a valid GTIN, your listing won't appear in search. If you're a reseller, get GTINs directly from manufacturer databases. Private label products need their own assigned GTINs; if building your own brand is the goal, our Walmart private label guide covers that path end to end.

Bulk Upload vs. Single Listing vs. API

  • Bulk CSV upload: Upload 10-10,000 SKUs at once. Fastest for initial catalog load. Supports Walmart's exact column format.
  • Single listing: Manual entry for 1-10 products. Slow but useful for testing category placement.
  • API integration: For resellers with hundreds/thousands of SKUs, API integration with Ecom Circles syncs inventory, pricing, and orders automatically.

Title & Description Optimization

Walmart's algorithm favors clarity over keyword density.

Good title example:

"Stainless Steel Water Bottle 32oz Insulated BPA-Free with Handle"

Include: primary keyword (what it is), key attributes (size, material, color), and benefit (insulated). Avoid: keyword stuffing, brand keywords you don't own, fake claims.

Images & Product Data Quality

Walmart requires minimum 4 product images (main + 3 variations). Use high-contrast backgrounds, show size context (person holding item), and include lifestyle/use-case shots. Add all product attributes: dimensions, weight, color options, materials. Complete data = higher search ranking + more conversions.

Reseller tip: Use Ecom Circles' Chrome extension to quickly validate GTINs, check competitor pricing, and verify category placement before uploading 100+ products. Catches common errors before they impact search visibility.

Fulfillment Options: Seller-Fulfilled vs. WFS

Option 1: Seller-Fulfilled

You handle: Inventory storage, packing, shipping, returns processing.

Best For:

  • • Tight-margin products (under $15 profit/unit)
  • • Oversized or heavy items (WFS costs prohibitive)
  • • Sellers with existing 3PL or warehouse
  • • Low-volume sellers (under 100 orders/month)

Advantages:

  • • Lower total costs (no WFS fees)
  • • Full control over packaging and branding
  • • Inventory flexibility (no storage minimums)

Requirements to Maintain Eligibility:

  • • On-time delivery rate 95%+ (ship within 2 days of order)
  • • Order cancellation rate under 1.5%
  • • Valid tracking on 100% of shipments
  • • Free returns (no return shipping charges)

Option 2: Walmart Fulfillment Services (WFS)

Walmart handles: Receiving, storage, picking, packing, shipping, returns.

Best For:

  • • Products with $20+ profit per unit
  • • Fast-moving inventory (sell out monthly)
  • • Sellers targeting 2-day delivery badge
  • • High-volume sellers (1000+ orders/month)

Advantages:

  • • 2-day delivery badge (major conversion boost)
  • • Pro Seller program eligibility
  • • No packing/shipping labor (full automated fulfillment)
  • • New-seller incentive: 25% off fulfillment + 50% off storage through Jan 2027 ($75K potential savings)

Costs:

  • • Fulfillment: ~$3.45 per standard unit (new sellers: $2.59)
  • • Monthly storage: $0.87-$2.60 per cubic foot (new sellers: $0.44-$1.30)
  • • Oversize fees apply for large/heavy items

Requirements for Eligibility:

  • • 90+ days active on Walmart (can accelerate after 30 days of high performance)
  • • 20+ orders in last 30 days
  • • 95%+ on-time delivery rate
  • • Under 2% order defect rate

Hybrid Strategy (Recommended for Resellers)

Use WFS for your top 20% of SKUs (fast-moving, $20+ profit margin) and seller-fulfilled for secondary inventory. This approach:

  • • Maximizes WFS new-seller discounts on best sellers
  • • Keeps margins healthy on slower items
  • • Qualifies you for Pro Seller badge (both methods mixed)
  • • Reduces overall costs while maintaining 2-day availability on top products

Pro Seller Badge Requirements (2026)

Walmart awards the Pro Seller Badge to top-performing sellers who maintain:

  • • 98%+ on-time delivery rate
  • • Under 1% order defect rate
  • • 99%+ valid tracking rate
  • • Under 0.5% cancellation rate
  • • Under 24 hour customer response time
  • • 90+ days on platform

Pro Seller Badge = increased search visibility, Buy Box preference, and Pro Seller program benefits (higher limits, advanced tools).

How to Maintain These Metrics

  • On-time delivery: Set realistic shipping times (1-2 days), integrate order management tools to catch delays early, and use tracking integration to reduce shipping errors.
  • Order defect rate: Ship correct items, process returns quickly, respond to defect claims within 48 hours, and maintain clear communication with customers.
  • Valid tracking: Always provide tracking numbers, use USPS/UPS/FedEx (Walmart verifies carriers), and update shipment status automatically via integration.
  • Fast response: Set up seller notifications on your phone, use pre-written templates for common questions, and assign a team member to monitor messages 24/7 (even for small sellers).

Scaling & Advanced Strategy: From $10K to $100K+/Month

Months 1-3: Foundation (First 100 Orders)

Focus on stability, not volume:

  • • List 50-100 SKUs in a narrow category (not 1000+ mixed products)
  • • Maintain 98%+ metrics (build cushion above Walmart's 95% threshold)
  • • Respond to every message within 2 hours (establish trust)
  • • Gather customer reviews (first 50 orders = critical for algorithm ranking)
  • • Monitor competitor pricing manually (before automating)

Months 4-6: Optimization ($10K-$30K/Month)

Once foundation is solid, optimize pricing and inventory:

  • Dynamic repricing: Implement Ecom Circles Repricer to auto-adjust prices based on competitor moves. Stay competitive without manual monitoring.
  • Inventory sync: If selling on Amazon + Walmart, sync inventory across platforms to avoid overselling.
  • 2-day delivery optimization: Shift top 20% of SKUs to WFS (high-velocity, $20+ profit). Leverage new-seller discounts.
  • Expand SKU depth: Add 100-200 more products in same/adjacent categories (leverage algorithm ranking).
  • Walmart Advertising (Connect): Test sponsored product ads on top 10 SKUs. Start with $5-10/day budget.

Months 7-12: Scaling ($30K-$100K+/Month)

Automate everything, focus on margin optimization:

  • Full WFS transition: If profit margins support it, move 60-80% of catalog to WFS. This unlocks Pro Seller eligibility and higher conversion rates.
  • Multi-channel inventory: Sell same products on Amazon + Walmart. Use Ecom Circles to manage both platforms from one dashboard.
  • API integration: Connect your inventory system (or supplier feeds) directly to Walmart via API. Eliminates manual uploads.
  • Increase SKU count strategically: Scale to 500-2000+ SKUs if your supply chain supports it. More SKUs = more search impressions = more sales.
  • Leverage auto-pricing and Auto Order: Use Ecom Circles' automation to handle bulk pricing updates, inventory reorders, and order preparation.
  • Supplier relationships: Negotiate volume discounts with suppliers as you scale. Every 10% reduction in COGS = 10% margin improvement.

The 90-Day Milestone

At 90 days on platform + 20+ orders + 95%+ metrics, you become eligible for WFS. This is the critical inflection point. Sellers who:

  • • Switch top 20% SKUs to WFS at 90 days see 30-50% conversion lift
  • • Get Pro Seller Badge (98%+ metrics) at 90 days see 50%+ traffic increase
  • • Maintain under $2 COGS + $20+ selling price see 35%+ net margin

Reaching $50K+/month typically takes 6-12 months of disciplined execution.

Multi-Marketplace Strategy: Walmart + Amazon Synergy

The highest-volume resellers don't pick one platform; they manage both:

  • Same inventory: Buy once, sell on both Walmart + Amazon. Diversifies risk, doubles revenue per purchase.
  • Platform differences: Use Amazon FBA for fast shipping, WFS on Walmart for cost savings. Adjust pricing per platform based on competitive landscape.
  • Data syncing: Track all sales, returns, and inventory in one system. Ecom Circles dashboard shows you aggregate performance across platforms.
  • Scaling efficiency: 100 SKUs on Walmart + 100 SKUs on Amazon = easier than 1000 SKUs on one platform. Managerial complexity stays the same, revenue doubles.
