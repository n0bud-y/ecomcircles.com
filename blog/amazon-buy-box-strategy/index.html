What is the Amazon Buy Box?

The Buy Box is the white box on the right side of a product detail page that displays the primary seller's offer. It's where customers add items to their cart. On products with multiple sellers, Amazon displays only one seller in the Buy Box at any time.

Amazon rotates the Buy Box among eligible sellers based on its algorithm. Winning the Buy Box is critical because 82% of FBA sales occur through it. Even if you rank #1 in search, losing the Buy Box tanks sales by 80-90%. And because Buy Box wins feed your sales velocity, they flow straight into improving your BSR on Amazon.

How Amazon's Buy Box Algorithm Works

Amazon doesn't publicly reveal the exact weighting, but a documented November 2025 algorithm update significantly shifted the weights. Delivery speed was elevated to 25-30% (from ~10% previously), while price was reduced to ~25% (from ~40-50%). This marks a fundamental recalibration toward speed and reliability over lowest cost. The same conversion signals also drive Amazon's cross-sell placements; see our breakdown of the Frequently Bought Together strategy for how to earn those.

FBA vs FBM: Buy Box Impact

FBA advantage has sharply diminished since November 2025. The algorithm is now fulfillment-channel agnostic, prioritizing delivery speed and seller metrics over fulfillment method. FBM sellers with Seller Fulfilled Prime (SFP). who can deliver 1-2 days, are now competitive for Buy Box. FBA remains advantageous in high-competition categories where speed is paramount, but an FBM seller with excellent metrics can outbid an FBA seller with poor performance.

FBA vs FBM: The 2025-2026 Reality

The "Prime Bump" advantage for FBA is now minimal. FBM sellers CAN win the Buy Box if they offer 1-2 day delivery to major population centers and maintain excellent seller metrics. The key competitive factor is delivery speed, not fulfillment method.
