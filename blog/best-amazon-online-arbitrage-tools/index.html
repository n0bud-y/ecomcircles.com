What Makes a Great Arbitrage Tool?

Before diving into specific tools, here's the criteria we use to evaluate them:

1. Speed. Can you upload a list of 100 products and get results in minutes (not hours)?

2. Accuracy. Do the profit calculations account for all fees (referral, FBA, shipping)?

3. Coverage. Does it check multiple marketplaces (Amazon + Walmart) or just one?

4. Restrictions. Does it flag restricted categories and IP issues before you buy?

5. Ease of use. Can you upload your own CSV format, or do you need to reformat everything?

6. Cost. Is the subscription justified by the time it saves?

Key Evaluation Questions

Before committing to a tool, ask yourself:

1. How many items will I scan per week?

  • 10-50 items: Helium 10 is fine (single-item lookup)
  • 50-200 items: Ecom Circles or Tactical Arbitrage (bulk upload)
  • 200+ items: Arbitrage Hero (speed is essential)

2. What sourcing channels do I use?

  • Retail only: Tactical Arbitrage
  • Retail + wholesale: Ecom Circles
  • Retail + wholesale + wholesale direct: Ecom Circles

3. Do I need Walmart coverage?

  • Amazon only: Helium 10 or Tactical Arbitrage
  • Amazon + Walmart: Ecom Circles

4. What's my monthly profit target?

  • <$2,000/mo: Budget tools (OABeans)
  • $2,000-$5,000/mo: Mid-tier (Tactical Arbitrage, Ecom Circles Growth)
  • $5,000+/mo: Premium (Helium 10 + dedicated scanner)

Pro Tips for Tool Usage

1. Automate your sourcing process

Most arbitrage sellers manually check retailers daily. Instead:

  • Sign up for retailer deal alerts (Walmart price drops, Target clearance feeds)
  • Use deal aggregators (CheapYetti curates by category)
  • Set up RSS feeds from retailer clearance pages
  • Load all deals into your scanner once per week

Result: 20 hours → 3 hours per week.

2. Combine tools strategically

Use bulk scanners to filter (find profitable deals), then use detailed analysts to validate (confirm demand, competition, margins).

  1. Upload 200 items to Ecom Circles → get 50 profitable results
  2. Take top 20 and validate in Helium 10 → confirm sales velocity
  3. Buy the 12 most validated deals

3. Monitor your hit rate

Track what percentage of sourced products actually sell profitably. If it's <60%, your filtering isn't aggressive enough. Tighten margin thresholds or add more validation.

4. Keep your sourcing formula simple

  • Only source products with 30%+ margin + 500+ monthly searches
  • Only source 20-30% of deals you identify
  • Source consistently (same volume every week), not in bursts
