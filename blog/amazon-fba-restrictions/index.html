Amazon FBA Restrictions 2026: Products to Avoid

Amazon restricts ~200 product categories/types from FBA. Major restrictions: hazardous materials (flammable, corrosive, toxic), weapons, drugs, counterfeit...

TL;DR (Quick List)

Amazon restricts ~200 product categories/types from FBA. Major restrictions: hazardous materials (flammable, corrosive, toxic), weapons, drugs, counterfeit goods, sexually explicit items, items requiring shipping labels/warnings. Before listing ANY product on FBA, check: (1) Category compliance via Seller Central, (2) Hazmat classification, (3) Brand/trademark ownership, (4) Gating requirements. Violation = account suspension. Related: What Is Amazon FNSKU?

Why FBA Restrictions Matter

Amazon has strict policies about what can be fulfilled through their network. These aren't arbitrary, and they're based on:

  • Safety: Hazardous materials can't be stored/shipped with standard packages
  • Legal compliance: Weapons, drugs, age-restricted items have legal restrictions
  • Trademark/IP: Counterfeit goods violate intellectual property
  • Logistics: Some items require special handling Amazon doesn't provide

Violating these restrictions = account suspension. Amazon doesn't give warnings. One hazmat shipment = review and possible permanent ban.

Category-Level Restrictions

Some entire product categories are FBA-restricted:

Hazardous Materials (HAZMAT)

Explicitly banned from FBA:

  • Flammable liquids and aerosols (paint, lighter fluid, propane)
  • Corrosive substances (bleach, drain cleaner, acid)
  • Toxic/poisonous materials (pesticides, rat poison)
  • Explosives
  • Oxidizers
  • Compressed gases
  • Biohazard materials
  • Radioactive materials

Why: Amazon's fulfillment centers aren't licensed for hazmat. Shipping hazmat requires special licensing, packaging, and documentation.

Workaround: Sell hazmat products as FBM (you fulfill). Requires your own hazmat compliance.

Weapons & Dangerous Items

FBA-restricted:

  • Firearms and ammunition
  • Knives (most categories, with exceptions for kitchen knives under strict conditions)
  • Explosives, fireworks, flares
  • Martial arts weapons (nunchucks, brass knuckles)
  • Archery bows

Why: Legal liability and safety concerns.

Workaround: Some knife and sporting goods categories can be FBA if you meet gating requirements. Kitchen knives are generally allowed.

Age-Restricted Products

FBA-restricted (or gated):

  • Alcoholic beverages (wine, beer, spirits)
  • Tobacco and tobacco alternatives
  • Sexually explicit materials
  • Pharmaceuticals and prescription items

Why: Legal age verification required. FBA automation can't verify buyer age.

Workaround: Sell as FBM if you can verify age of buyer (manually).

Counterfeit or Intellectual Property Violations

FBA-restricted (and account-ending):

  • Counterfeit branded goods
  • Items infringing trademarks
  • Unauthorized replicas
  • Items with unauthorized brand logos

Why: Amazon aggressively protects brand owners. Trademark complaints result in permanent suspension.

The hardest part: Sometimes it's not obvious. Branded vs. knockoff can be gray.

Examples:

  • ❌ Selling "Nike-style" running shoes with Nike branding = counterfeit = suspension
  • ✅ Selling generic running shoes without Nike branding = fine
  • ❌ Selling "Coach" bags from unverified sellers = likely counterfeit
  • ✅ Selling "Coach" bags as authorized reseller with brand approval = fine

Product-Level Restrictions

Beyond categories, specific product attributes can trigger FBA restrictions:

Batteries & Battery-Powered Items

Restricted: Lithium batteries, products with lithium batteries (power banks, laptops, phones)

Why: Air shipping regulations. Lithium batteries are Class 9 hazmat.

Workaround: Some battery products are FBA-eligible if properly labeled and from approved suppliers. Check with Seller Central.

Food & Beverages

FBA-restricted: Most food and beverages require special licensing.

Exceptions:

  • Shelf-stable snacks in sealed, original packaging
  • Pet food (non-prescription)
  • Vitamins/supplements (non-drug)

Why: FDA oversight and contamination liability.

Jewelry & Watches

Often gated (restricted to approved sellers):

  • Designer/luxury watches
  • High-value jewelry
  • Replica/counterfeit jewelry

Why: Resale legitimacy concerns and trademark protection.

Electronics

Some gated (restricted):

  • Refurbished electronics (requires certification)
  • Used electronics (condition verification required)
  • Open-box electronics

Why: Amazon protects buyers from counterfeit or broken electronics.

Gating: The Middleground

Some categories aren't outright FBA-restricted, but "gated" - meaning you must request approval before selling. The flip side is worth knowing too: our guide to ungated categories on Amazon lists where you can start selling with no approval at all.

Common Gated Categories:

  • Automotive parts
  • Beauty products (brand approval often required)
  • Collectibles
  • Fine art
  • Jewelry
  • Watches
  • Shoes/apparel (luxury brands)
  • Wheels & tires
  • Premium electronics (certain brands)

How Gating Works:

  1. Try to list product in category
  2. Amazon shows "This category is restricted"
  3. Click "Request Approval"
  4. Provide:
  5. Business documents (tax ID, resale certificate)
  6. Brand authorization (if selling branded products)
  7. Product documentation
  8. Source documentation (where you buy from supplier)
  9. Amazon reviews and approves (or denies)
  10. Approval typically takes 1-3 weeks

Once approved: You can sell unlimited products in that category.

How to Check If a Product Is FBA-Restricted

Method 1: Check Seller Central

  1. Log into Seller Central
  2. Click Inventory → Add a Product
  3. Search for your product (by ASIN, UPC, or keyword)
  4. Try to select "Fulfilled by Amazon"
  5. If restricted, Amazon will show error message: "This category requires approval" or "This product type is not eligible for FBA"

Method 2: Search Amazon's Restriction List

Amazon maintains a list of FBA-restricted categories:

  1. Go to Seller Central
  2. Click Performance → FBA Restrictions
  3. View full list of restricted categories and products

Method 3: Check the Product Detail Page

On Amazon's storefront:

  1. Find the product you want to sell
  2. Look at fulfillment method of existing sellers
  3. If all sellers use FBM, it's likely FBA-restricted
  4. If some sellers use FBA (Prime badge), it's probably FBA-eligible

Red Flags: Products to Avoid for FBA

🚩 Hazmat Classification

Test: Does the product have a safety data sheet (SDS)? Does it require special shipping labels?

If yes → FBA-restricted

Examples:

  • Air fresheners (sometimes flammable)
  • Cleaning supplies
  • Paints and solvents
  • Batteries
  • Lithium-ion power banks

🚩 Requires Age Verification

Test: Is this product age-restricted in most states? Can Amazon verify age without human verification?

If yes → FBA-restricted or heavily gated

Examples:

  • Alcohol
  • Tobacco
  • Vaping products
  • Adult content

🚩 Counterfeit Risk

Test: Is this a branded product you don't have authorization to sell? Is it a designer knockoff?

If yes → Don't sell at all (permanent account risk)

Examples:

  • "Fake" designer bags
  • Counterfeit electronics
  • Unlicensed brand merchandise

🚩 Requires Special Handling

Test: Does this product require signature on delivery, special packaging, or customs declarations?

If yes → Potentially FBA-restricted

Examples:

  • High-value items (jewelry, watches)
  • Collectibles
  • Items requiring customs documentation

🚩 Food or Health Claims

Test: Does the product make health or medical claims? Is it regulated by FDA?

If yes → Likely restricted

Examples:

  • Products claiming to "cure" or "treat" diseases
  • Prescription items
  • Unlabeled dietary supplements

What Happens If You Violate FBA Restrictions

Severity 1: Soft Warning

Trigger: Listing one product in a restricted category

Amazon response: Rejection message. Product won't list. No account impact.

Action: Remove and don't repeat.

Severity 2: Category Deactivation

Trigger: Repeated attempts to list in restricted categories

Amazon response: Category disabled. Can't list in that category. Account still active.

Action: Request approval for category (if applicable) or move on.

Severity 3: FBA Suspension

Trigger: Shipping hazmat or violating major FBA restrictions

Amazon response: All FBA privileges revoked. Can still sell via FBM, but not FBA.

Timeline: Immediate to 24 hours

Recovery: Contact seller support, demonstrate compliance improvement, request reinstatement (usually 1-2 weeks)

Severity 4: Account Suspension

Trigger: Counterfeit goods, severe safety violations, repeated hazmat shipping

Amazon response: Entire seller account closed. All inventory delisted. Funds held for 90+ days.

Recovery: Rare. Requires lawyer and proof of compliance overhaul.

How to Stay Compliant

Before Listing Any Product:

  1. Check the restriction list in Seller Central
  2. Test FBA eligibility by attempting to create listing
  3. Verify brand/trademark ownership if selling branded products
  4. Check hazmat classification using supplier SDS (Safety Data Sheet)
  5. Review product reviews to see fulfillment method other sellers use

Before Your First FBA Shipment:

  1. Confirm with Amazon that product is approved for FBA
  2. Get written approval from supplier that product complies with Amazon's safety standards
  3. Verify packaging/labeling requirements (especially post-March 31, 2026 FNSKU requirement)
  4. Test with small shipment (100 units) and monitor for rejections

Ongoing Compliance:

  1. Monitor policy changes: Amazon updates restrictions quarterly
  2. Join Seller Central notifications to get alerts about new restrictions
  3. Check on your account periodically for warnings from Amazon
  4. Don't source from unverified suppliers: counterfeit risk increases with sketchy suppliers
