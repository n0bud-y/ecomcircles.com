Supercharge your Amazon selling with the best Chrome extensions. Compare Keepa, Jungle Scout, Helium 10, AMZScout, the EC Seller Tools extension, and more. Speed up research, optimize pricing, and make smarter decisions right from your browser.

Why Chrome Extensions Matter for Amazon Sellers in 2026

Chrome extensions are the fastest way to evaluate a product without leaving the Amazon page. For resellers, whether you're scanning clearance aisles for retail arbitrage, sourcing wholesale deals, or hunting online arbitrage. extensions overlay critical data like price history, FBA fees, IP risk alerts, and profit margins directly on the product listing. No context-switching. No spreadsheets. Instant go/no-go decisions.

The 2026 extension landscape is shifting. Helium 10 dropped its Starter plan and raised prices, pushing budget-conscious sellers toward alternatives like Jungle Scout and specialized arbitrage tools. Meanwhile, Walmart-focused extensions remain scarce, which is exactly where EC Seller Tools fills the gap. If you sell on both Amazon and Walmart, you'll likely need at least two extensions.

Below, we review the top 8 Chrome extensions for Amazon sellers, ranked by value for resellers doing RA, OA, and wholesale, not private label product launchers.

2. Keepa

The gold standard for price history, and an essential tool for every reseller. Keepa embeds historical price charts, sales rank trends, Buy Box history, and stock levels directly on Amazon product pages. For RA and OA sellers, Keepa charts are how you distinguish a genuine deal from a temporary price spike. Rated 4.7/5 with over 5,000 reviews.

Best For:

  • Price history analysis
  • Seasonal pricing patterns
  • Retail arbitrage hunting

Key Features:

  • Detailed price charts
  • Free tier available
  • Multi-marketplace support

Pricing: Free (with premium at $19.99/month)

3. Jungle Scout Chrome Extension

Comprehensive product research extension with competitor tracking, profit calculator, and market insights. In head-to-head accuracy tests, Jungle Scout won 13 out of 20 comparisons against Helium 10, making it the more reliable option for sales estimates. The free extension provides basic insights, with the Pro version unlocking advanced features.

Best For:

  • Product opportunity analysis
  • Competitor benchmarking
  • Estimated monthly sales

Key Features:

  • Profit calculator
  • Sales estimator
  • Review analysis

Pricing: Included with Jungle Scout subscription (from $99/month)

4. Helium 10 Chrome Extension

Full-featured extension with ASIN analysis, keyword research, competitor tracking, and listing optimization insights.

Note: Helium 10 discontinued its Starter plan in 2026 and raised prices on remaining tiers. Paid plans now start at $39/month (Platinum). Their Trustpilot rating has dropped to 2.7/5, with common complaints about customer service and pricing. Despite this, the Xray tool remains one of the most data-rich product research extensions available.

Best For:

  • Keyword research
  • ASIN deep dives
  • Listing optimization

Key Features:

  • Cerebro integration
  • Trend analysis
  • Competitor monitoring

Pricing: Included with Helium 10 subscription (from $99/month)
