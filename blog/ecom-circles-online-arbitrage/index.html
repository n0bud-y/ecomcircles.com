Online arbitrage -buying products from retailers and reselling on marketplaces -is a proven income model. But it's painful to scale manually.

You're constantly checking retail prices, calculating margins, updating listings, managing inventory across marketplaces, tracking profits by product, and dealing with returns. Every step is manual. Every step is a bottleneck.

Ecom Circles was built for sellers like you. This guide shows how Ecom Circles handles each part of your operation and why it matters for arbitrage at scale.

The Online Arbitrage Challenge

Online arbitrage sounds simple: buy low, sell high. In practice, it's a coordination nightmare.

Manual workflow:

  1. Check Walmart/Amazon/Costco for deals
  2. Calculate margin (price minus cost minus fees)
  3. List on your marketplace (Amazon, eBay, Shopify)
  4. Wait for sales
  5. Buy the product from retail store
  6. Ship to customer
  7. Process returns
  8. Track profitability per SKU
  9. Repeat for 20+ products simultaneously

Problems:

  • Prices change constantly; your spreadsheet is out of date
  • You miss arbitrage windows (price drop for 2 hours, then back up)
  • Inventory conflicts (sell on Amazon and eBay, oversell)
  • Manual shipping is slow (customer orders Monday, you buy and ship Wednesday = low ratings)
  • Margins are buried (profit per unit is unclear)
  • Scaling is impossible without hiring staff

This is where Ecom Circles enters.

How Ecom Circles Handles Sourcing

Sourcing Scanner: Find Profitable Products in Bulk

Instead of manually checking products one-by-one, you upload a list (CSV or XLSX) of products you're considering. The scanner:

  • Matches against Amazon/Walmart simultaneously - shows current prices on both platforms
  • Calculates real profit margins - includes all marketplace fees, processing fees, and shipping
  • Checks restrictions - alerts you to gated categories and IP risk before you buy
  • Identifies sales velocity - shows estimated demand so you know if a product will move
  • Requires no reformatting - your spreadsheet works as-is (this is critical for arbitrage, where you're pulling lists from various sources)

Example workflow:

  1. Find 50 clearance items on Walmart
  2. Export list: Title, ASIN, UPC, Cost
  3. Upload to Ecom Circles Scanner (takes 30 seconds)
  4. Scanner returns: Current prices on Amazon + Walmart, fees, calculated margin, demand estimate, restrictions
  5. Filter for 20%+ margins
  6. Launch those products

Time saved: 2 hours of manual research reduced to 10 minutes.

Built-In Margin Calculations

Most sellers guess at margins. Ecom Circles calculates real margins:

  • Considers all marketplace fees (referral, fulfillment, payment processing)
  • Accounts for shipping costs
  • Includes platform-specific rules (Amazon's higher fees, Walmart's lower fees)
  • Shows net profit per unit, not gross margin

Example:

You list a $30 product on Amazon FBM. Your manual calculation:

  • Selling price: $30
  • Cost: $12
  • Profit: $18 (60% margin!)

Reality with Ecom Circles:

  • Selling price: $30
  • Sourcing cost: -$12
  • Amazon referral fee: -$4.50
  • Shipping cost: -$1.80
  • Payment processing: -$0.90
  • Returns (5% risk): -$1.50
  • Real profit: $9.30 (31% margin)

This transparency prevents you from listing unprofitable products.

How Ecom Circles Handles Inventory Management

Multi-Channel Inventory Sync

You sell on Amazon and eBay. A customer buys on Amazon. Seconds later, you sell the same product on eBay to another customer. Now you need to buy 2 units but the retail store is out of stock.

Overselling destroys reputation and margins.

Ecom Circles prevents this by:

  • Syncing inventory automatically across all channels
  • Consolidating stock numbers (if you have 5 units, that's your limit across Amazon, eBay, and Shopify combined)
  • Alerting you before overselling (shows "only 2 units available" before you sell the 3rd)
  • Flagging low-stock items so you know when to reorder

COGS Tracking

You need to know your true cost on every product. Arbitrage costs vary (you bought this product at Walmart for $8, that one at Costco for $6, another on clearance for $4).

Ecom Circles lets you set COGS per SKU. Then every margin calculation is accurate, and your tax accounting is correct.

Bulk Edit and Sync

When prices change (or you want to adjust pricing), you can:

  • Upload a CSV with price changes
  • Bulk edit 50+ products at once
  • Sync changes to all marketplaces in one step

Instead of manually listing each product on each platform, you list once and sync everywhere.

How Ecom Circles Handles Repricing

Automatic Price Adjustments

Your arbitrage windows close fast. A Walmart item costs $8 today and $12 tomorrow. You need to adjust your listing price before someone else undercuts you.

Ecom Circles Repricer automatically adjusts prices based on your rules:

  • Get the Buy Box (match competitor pricing within X cents)
  • Maintain margin (keep profit at 25%+ even if competitor goes lower)
  • Velocity pricing (lower price to move slow-moving stock)

This is live and running. You set rules once, and prices adjust hourly.

Example:

  • You list a product at $25
  • A competitor enters at $22
  • Your repricer automatically drops to $21.50 (undercut by $0.50)
  • Competitor drops to $19
  • Your repricer stops (hits minimum margin threshold)
  • You pause that product; profit isn't worth it

Manual vs. automated:

  • Manual: You check 20 products, adjust 8 of them, update listings, hope you didn't miss any. Takes 1 hour/day.
  • Ecom Circles: Rules run 24/7. You spend 30 minutes setting up rules, then check in weekly to see what's happening.

How Ecom Circles Handles Returns

For arbitrage, returns are painful. A customer returns a product. You need to:

  • Refund the customer
  • Deal with the returned product (resell, liquidate, or donate)
  • Track that this product has a high return rate

Ecom Circles helps by:

  • Tracking return rates by product (show which items are problematic)
  • Centralizing returns across marketplaces (see all returns in one inbox)
  • Recording return reasons (defect? Not as described? Unwanted?)

This data helps you decide: is this product worth relisting? Should I adjust pricing or description to manage expectations?
