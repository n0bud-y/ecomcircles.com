What Is AMZ Seller Browser?

AMZ Seller Browser is a paid Chrome extension designed specifically for sellers already in Seller Central. When you visit a competitor's listing or your own, the extension shows:

  • Estimated sales (units sold per month)
  • Price history (how prices have trended)
  • Profit calculator (revenue minus estimated fees)
  • Review insights (average rating, review velocity)
  • Competitor tracking (who else is selling this product)

Pricing: ~$49/month (estimated; exact pricing may vary)

Current Status: Still active in 2026, but users commonly search for alternatives due to feature limitations and competition from stronger platforms.

AMZ Seller Browser Strengths

1. Works Inside Seller Central

Unlike standalone tools that require separate logins, AMZ Seller Browser integrates directly with Seller Central. You see metrics right where you're already working.

2. Quick Price & Sales Lookups

For spotting trends on a specific listing, it's fast. Click the extension icon, see estimated sales and pricing history instantly.

3. Focused Design

It does one thing (Seller Central enrichment) without overwhelming UI. Good for sellers who don't want tool bloat.

AMZ Seller Browser Limitations

1. Limited Research Features

AMZ Seller Browser shows metrics on listings you visit, but doesn't help you discover new niches or products. You can't bulk-analyze product categories.

2. No Repricing

You can see price history, but you can't set up automatic repricing. You'll need another tool to stay competitive.

3. No Inventory Management

Stock tracking, low-stock alerts, reorder forecasting, not included.

4. No Multi-Channel Support

If you sell on Walmart too, AMZ Seller Browser doesn't help. You'd need separate tools for Walmart.

5. Limited Integration

It works inside Seller Central, but doesn't integrate with your inventory software, repricing tool, or advertising platform.

6. Outdated Compared to Modern Platforms

Helium 10 and Jungle Scout now offer similar inline metrics PLUS research, repricing, inventory, and advertising. For the same price, you get far more.

✅ You only care about quick inline metrics while browsing Seller Central ✅ You have a tool stack you love and just want Seller Central enrichment ✅ You're on a tight budget and don't need repricer/inventory/research
