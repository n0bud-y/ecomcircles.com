This is our own review of Ecom Circles. We're not hiding weaknesses. You'll see what we do better than competitors and where we're still catching up. We built this platform to solve real seller problems, but we're not perfect, and we're honest about it.

What Is Ecom Circles?

Ecom Circles is an all-in-one platform for Amazon and Walmart resellers and wholesale suppliers. It combines product sourcing (via our scanner), repricing automation, inventory management, order handling, and an in-house warehouse with fulfillment services. Think of it as one dashboard to manage your entire reseller operation across multiple marketplaces.

We serve 7,174+ sellers managing over $1.7B in product volume, 5.15M active listings, and processing 1.29M orders. If you're doing reselling or wholesale arbitrage across Amazon and Walmart, Ecom Circles handles the repetitive, time-consuming parts so you can focus on strategy.

Key Features Breakdown

Scanner: Find Wholesale Products at Scale

Our scanner pulls millions of products from wholesale suppliers (Alibaba, Global Sources, etc.). You set your target margin, and it surfaces deals you can arbitrage on Amazon and Walmart. It runs continuously so you're not manually searching every day.

What it does: Scans 5M+ wholesale SKUs, filters by profit margin, shows pricing history, competitor listings, and demand signals.

Honest limitation: No Google Sheets export yet (coming soon). If you need to pull data into your own workflows, that's not available right now.

Repricer: Auto-Adjust Prices to Win

The repricer automatically adjusts your prices to match Buy Box conditions, competitor pricing, and inventory levels. It's passive, set it once and let it work while you sleep.

What it does: Monitors competitor prices hourly, adjusts your prices to maintain Buy Box or stay competitive, respects your floor/ceiling price limits.

Honest limitation: Currently, only 1 strategy (Buy Box repricing) is live. We have 3 more repricing strategies coming soon (min-margin, velocity-based, demand-based). If you need complex multi-factor repricing right now, you might hit a ceiling.

Inventory Management: Stock Across Warehouses

Centralized inventory tracking across FBA, FBM, Walmart WFS, and our own 3PL warehouse. One place to see SKU stock levels, warehouse location, and movement.

What it does: Automatic sync with Amazon, Walmart, and our warehouse systems. Prevents overselling, tracks stock by warehouse, reports aging inventory.

Honest limitation: No AI-powered demand forecasting. No user-configurable reorder points per SKU. This is a tracking system, not a predictive one.

Order Management: One Dashboard, All Orders

All your marketplace orders. Amazon, Walmart, Mirakl, in one place. Track shipments, manage returns, handle refunds, print labels.

What it does: Aggregates orders from all marketplaces, shows fulfillment status, integrates shipping labels, surfaces returns and chargebacks.

Honest limitation: No one-click fulfillment auto-routing yet. No per-order team assignment. If your team is large and needs granular order delegation, you'll need external tools or manual routing.

Browser Extension: Quick Lookups on Amazon & Walmart

When you're browsing Amazon or Walmart product pages, the extension shows your cost basis (from our scanner), your current price, competitor prices, and historical pricing.

What it does: One-click insights without leaving the marketplace. Check your profit margin on any product.

Honest limitation: Sales estimates shown in the extension are NOT AI-powered. They're based on price history and static demand signals, not machine learning.

In-House Warehouse & Fulfillment

We operate a 3PL warehouse. You can store inventory with us, and we handle prep (FBA/WFS labeling), storage, and outbound fulfillment to your customers.

Pricing: FBA/WFS prep $1/unit • Outbound fulfillment $3/order • Oversize items $1.50/unit • Heavyweight items $2.50/unit

Competitive advantage: Most reseller tools don't touch fulfillment. This is where we're differentiated.

Who Should Look Elsewhere

Private Label Product Researchers

If you're deep in product research before launching a brand, tools like Helium 10 or Jungle Scout have better keyword research, demand estimation, and competition analysis.

Single-Product Sellers

If you have one product on Amazon and that's it, you don't need most of Ecom Circles. Keepa's basic pricing tool might be cheaper.

Amazon-Only Sellers (No Walmart Plans)

If you're purely on Amazon and never plan to expand to Walmart, Helium 10 or Jungle Scout might offer better specialization for your use case.

Sellers Needing Advanced Repricing Today

Honest Limitations

  • Repricer: 1 strategy live. Buy Box repricing works well. Demand-based, min-margin, and velocity-based strategies are coming, but not here yet.
  • Scanner: No Google Sheets export. You can view scans in the app, but there's no bulk export to Google Sheets for further analysis. Coming soon.
  • Extension: Sales estimates aren't AI-powered. They're based on historical price and basic demand signals, not machine learning models.
  • Inventory: No forecasting or reorder automation. You see current stock levels, but there's no AI-driven reorder point suggestions.
  • Order management: No auto-routing. You manage order assignments manually or via rules. No per-order team delegation yet.
  • Primarily built for resellers/wholesale. If you're doing deep private label product research, other tools are better suited.
