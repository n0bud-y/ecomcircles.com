How to Open an Amazon Seller Account in 2026: Complete Setup Guide

Your first decision is account type. This choice impacts fees, available features, and scalability.

TL;DR

Open an Amazon seller account in 15 minutes: verify email, provide personal/business info, add payment method, and choose Individual or Professional plan. The 2026 registration process requires government ID verification and phone confirmation. Individual accounts sell without monthly fees but with 15% referral fees and limited tools. Professional accounts cost $39.99/month but enable FBA, advertising, and brand registry (worth the investment if selling 30+ units/month). This guide covers step-by-step setup, account types, requirements, and how to avoid common approval issues.

Individual vs. Professional Seller Account

Your first decision is account type. This choice impacts fees, available features, and scalability.

Individual Account

  • Cost: Free (no monthly subscription)
  • Per-unit fee: $0.99 per item sold (in addition to referral fee)
  • Referral fee: 8-45% (varies by category)
  • Total per sale: $0.99 + referral fee
  • Monthly sales limit: Effectively unlimited, but losing $0.99 per item is expensive at scale

Available features:

  • ✓ Basic selling (FBM or FBA)
  • ✗ Sponsored Ads (PPC): No access
  • ✗ Brand Registry: No access
  • ✗ Seller Central reports: Limited
  • ✗ A+ Content: No access
  • ✓ FBA available

Best for:

  • Testing if selling on Amazon is viable
  • Casual side hustlers selling <10 items/month
  • Quick cash from clearing inventory

Real cost example (Individual):

  • 30 items/month @ $20 average price
  • Referral fee (assume 15%): $90
  • Per-unit fee: $30
  • Total fees: $120 (total monthly take-home: $480 at 50% margins)

Professional Account

  • Cost: $39.99/month (can cancel anytime)
  • Per-unit fee: $0 (no per-item charge)
  • Referral fee: 8-45% (same as Individual)
  • Total per sale: Just referral fee
  • Breakeven: ~40 items/month (where per-unit savings offset $39.99 subscription)

Available features:

  • ✓ Sponsored Ads (PPC): Required for growth
  • ✓ Brand Registry: Protects brand, enables A+ Content
  • ✓ Advanced Seller Central reports
  • ✓ A+ Content: Conversion boost
  • ✓ Bulk uploading: Upload 100+ listings at once
  • ✓ FBA (optimal for ranking)

Best for:

  • Serious sellers targeting $1,000+/month revenue
  • Multi-product portfolios
  • Anyone planning to scale

Real cost example (Professional):

  • 30 items/month @ $20 average price
  • Monthly subscription: $39.99
  • Referral fee: $90
  • Total fees: $130 (same take-home as Individual, but with far more features and higher ceiling)

Recommendation: Start with Individual for testing (1-2 products). If you hit 30+ items/month, upgrade to Professional (it pays for itself in per-item fee savings alone, plus you unlock PPC and brand registry).

Step-by-Step Registration Process (2026)

Step 1: Go to Amazon Seller Central

  1. Navigate to: https://sellercentral.amazon.com/
  2. Click "Create your Amazon Seller Account"
  3. You'll be prompted to sign in with Amazon account (or create new)

Note: Use a dedicated email address for seller account (don't use your personal Amazon buyer account email).

Step 2: Account Type Selection

  1. Choose Individual or Professional
  2. Click "Continue"

Step 3: Enter Your Information

Personal Information:

  • Full name
  • Email address (verified via confirmation link)
  • Phone number (you'll receive verification code via SMS)
  • Address (business or personal)

Business Information:

  • Store name (your brand name on Amazon)
  • Business type: Sole proprietor, LLC, Corporation, Other
  • Taxpayer ID (SSN if sole proprietor; EIN if business)

Step 4: Government ID Verification (NEW in 2026)

Amazon now requires government ID verification for all new sellers.

  1. Have ready: Driver's license, passport, or national ID
  2. Take clear photos (front and back)
  3. Upload via Seller Central
  4. Amazon verifies (typically 1-3 business days)

Note: This is automatic; if your ID doesn't match your account name/address, you may be rejected and asked to resubmit.

Step 5: Payment Method Setup

  1. Add credit/debit card for:
  2. Monthly subscription (Professional only)
  3. Refunds to customers
  4. Verify via small charge ($0.01-$1.00) that's credited back

Step 6: Additional Settings

  1. Store name: Choose how your brand appears in search and on your listings
  2. Phone number: Confirm phone (used for two-factor authentication)
  3. Tax information: If applicable, provide tax ID for sales tax collection

Step 7: Confirmation

You'll receive confirmation email. Your account is now live and ready to create listings.

Typical timeline: 5-30 minutes for manual setup, +1-3 days for ID verification.

Account Requirements and Restrictions

2026 Seller Registration Requirements

Age & Identity:

  • Must be 18+ (or emancipated minor)
  • Government ID required (matches account holder name)
  • Phone verification mandatory

Location:

  • Based in approved country (US, UK, Germany, France, Spain, Italy, Japan, Canada, Australia, etc.)
  • Physical address required (no PO boxes for seller address)

Banking:

  • Valid bank account for deposits (not required at signup, but needed before first sale)
  • Payouts occur every 14 days

Categories with Extra Requirements:

Some categories require additional approval (gating):

  • Electronics: No gating for most (but high-return categories require seller history)
  • Supplements: Requires DSHEA compliance documentation
  • Topicals/beauty products: May require certifications
  • Food/grocery: Requires supplier documentation
  • Liquor, jewelry, watches: Restricted; requires approval process

You can sell in ungated categories immediately upon account approval.

Common Registration Issues and Fixes

Issue 1: "We could not verify your address"

Cause: Address doesn't match government ID or is flagged as invalid.

Fix:

  • Use address exactly as it appears on your government ID
  • Ensure street format matches (e.g., "Street" vs. "St.", "Road" vs. "Rd.")
  • If you've recently moved, update your government ID first

Issue 2: "Account suspended due to unverified identity"

Cause: ID verification failed (photo unclear, name mismatch, or document expired).

Fix:

  • Retake clear photos of both sides of ID
  • Verify all text is readable
  • Ensure ID is valid (not expired)
  • Contact Seller Support with clear photos

If the suspension sticks, or your account gets deactivated again after launch, work through our Amazon account suspended emergency checklist.

Issue 3: "Professional account subscription declined"

Cause: Payment method failed (expired card, incorrect billing address, fraud hold).

Fix:

  • Update credit card on file
  • Verify billing address matches card
  • Contact bank to unlock potential fraud hold

Issue 4: "Category is gated; you must apply for approval"

Cause: Your chosen category requires seller history or documentation.

Fix:

  • Choose ungated categories first (Home, Sports, Toys, etc.)
  • Build 30+ sales history
  • Apply for gating after 30 days with clean metrics
  • Or provide required documentation (certificates, invoices, business license)

Post-Registration: First Steps to Launch

Step 1: Complete Your Seller Profile (1-2 hours)

  1. Store information:
  2. Add store logo and description
  3. Add store policies (shipping, returns, warranty)
  1. Bank account setup:
  2. Add bank account for payouts
  3. Verify via micro-deposits (2 small deposits Amazon sends; you confirm amounts)
  1. Tax settings (if applicable):
  2. Add sales tax collection if required in your states
  3. Add VAT if international

Step 2: Add Payment Method and Verify Account (10 minutes)

  1. Go to Seller Central → Account info → Payment method
  2. Add credit card
  3. Verify via small charge

Step 3: Create Your First Listing (20-30 minutes)

  1. Go to Seller Central → Inventory → Add Products
  2. Search for existing product or create new
  3. Fill in product details (title, images, description, etc.)
  4. Set price and quantity
  5. Publish

For detailed listing creation, see our guide on [high-converting Amazon listings].

Step 4: Configure Shipping Method (10 minutes)

Choose FBA (Amazon fulfills) or FBM (you fulfill):

  • FBA: Amazon stores, ships, handles returns (2-3% fulfillment fee)
  • FBM: You handle everything (full control, lower fees)

For your first product, FBM is simpler. Scale to FBA once you have 10+ listings.

Step 5: Verify Account and Get Approval to Sell (1-24 hours)

Amazon may require additional verification:

  • Email confirmation
  • Phone confirmation
  • ID re-verification

Once approved, you can begin selling immediately.

Professional Account: Worth It?

Break-even calculation:

At what sales volume does Professional ($39.99/month) pay for itself?

  • Individual: Per-item fee = $0.99
  • Professional: No per-item fee, just $39.99/month

Break-even = $39.99 ÷ $0.99 per item = 40 items/month

ROI analysis:

At 50 items/month @ $20 average price:

  • Individual cost: (50 × $0.99) + (referral fee) = $49.50 + referral fee
  • Professional cost: $39.99 + referral fee

Savings: $9.51/month (plus access to PPC, brand registry, A+ Content)

At this volume, Professional is worth it immediately. Upgrade as soon as you're profitable or have 30+ items/month.
