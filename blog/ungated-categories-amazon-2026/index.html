What Does "Ungated" Mean on Amazon?

Amazon gates categories to control who can sell products and ensure brand compliance, safety, and legal requirements. When a category is gated, only approved sellers can list new products in that category.

An ungated category is open to any professional seller. You can list new products immediately without needing brand approval or submitting documentation.

A gated category requires approval. Before listing, you must:

  • Get approval from the brand owner or trademark holder, or
  • Submit proof of authorization (invoices from manufacturer/wholesaler, resale certificates, licensing)

Gating protects brands from unauthorized sellers and prevents counterfeit products, IP violations, and liability issues.

Currently Gated Categories on Amazon (2026)

These categories are restricted and require approval to list new products:

Highly Restricted (Most Difficult to Get Approved)

  • Automotive (requires authorization from manufacturer or authorized distributor)
  • Supplements and Nutritional Products (requires lab testing, ingredient verification, FDA compliance)
  • Beauty and Personal Care (high risk, requires authorization, especially for color cosmetics)
  • Fine Watches and Jewelry (luxury items, brand protection)
  • Fine Art and Collectibles (authenticity and provenance verification required)
  • Luxury Handbags, Luggage, and Accessories (trademark and brand protection)
  • Shoes and Handbags (brand-gated to prevent counterfeits)
  • Wines and Spirits (regulatory requirements vary by state)
  • Weapons and Ammunition (federal licensing required)
  • Medications and Medical Devices (prescription-strength items)

Moderately Restricted (Approval More Attainable)

  • Baby Products (safety standards and certification required)
  • Health and Wellness (some categories require documentation)
  • Topicals and Skincare (depending on claims made)
  • Pet Food and Treats (certain restricted due to health claims)

Brand-Restricted (Approval From Brand Required)

  • Any category where a brand has enrolled in Brand Registry

How to Check If a Category Is Gated in 2026

Amazon displays gate status in Seller Central, and it's the source of truth.

Step 1: Log into Seller Central

Go to seller.amazon.com and sign in with your professional seller account.

Step 2: Navigate to Inventory

Click "Inventory" → "Add Products."

Step 3: Search for a Product (or Category)

Search by:

  • Product name or ASIN (to check an existing product)
  • Category (to check category-level gating)

Step 4: Check Gate Status

When you search, Amazon displays a gate notice if the category is restricted. For example: "This category is restricted. You need approval to add products to this category. [Apply for approval →]"

Step 5: Verify Before Investing

Check for gating BEFORE you source inventory. Never order 500 units of a product you can't list because you didn't check the category status first.

How to Get Ungated in Gated Categories

If you want to sell in a restricted category, you have two paths: brand approval or proof of authorization.

Path 1: Get Brand Approval

For brand-gated categories (luxury brands, trademark holders), contact the brand directly and request approval to sell their products on Amazon.

Steps:

  1. Find the brand's contact information (website, LinkedIn, or phone directory)
  2. Reach out to their brand protection or marketplace partner team
  3. Provide documentation: business registration, resale certificate, and proof of purchase from authorized distributors
  4. Wait for approval (typically 2-4 weeks)
  5. Forward the brand's approval to Amazon Seller Support to remove the gate

Path 2: Submit Proof of Authorization

For categories like supplements, automotive, or beauty, Amazon accepts invoices or certificates proving you're authorized to sell.

Required Documentation:

  • Invoices from manufacturers or authorized wholesalers (dated within last 180 days)
  • Resale certificates (for states that require them)
  • Product licensing or certifications (FDA registration for supplements, safety testing results for baby products)
  • Brand permission letters (if selling a branded product)

Steps:

  1. Go to Seller Central → "Account" → "Gating Request"
  2. Select the category and subcategory you want access to
  3. Upload documentation
  4. Wait for Amazon's review (typically 24 hours to 7 days)
  5. Amazon approves or requests additional documentation

2026 Category Status Changes & Updates

The ungating landscape has shifted slightly in 2026:

Baby Products: Remains gated but approval is more accessible. Amazon now accepts third-party safety certifications (CPSIA, ASTM) instead of requiring all products to be tested by Amazon's labs. This has opened baby products category to more sellers.

Supplements: Increasingly restricted. Amazon now requires GMP (Good Manufacturing Practice) certification and FDA facility registration for all supplement sellers, not just first-time applicants. This was implemented in Q1 2026.

Beauty and Personal Care: Brand-gated status is expanding. More beauty brands are enrolling in Brand Registry, which gates their categories to protect against counterfeits. If you sell popular brands (e.g., MAC, Olaplex), expect gating.

Automotive: Status unchanged. Still requires manufacturer authorization or authorized distributor invoices.

Pet Food: Conditional gating expanding. Wet pet food products are increasingly gated (veterinary restrictions), while dry kibble remains mostly ungated. Check category status before listing.

Shoes & Handbags: Brand-gated. Many luxury brands have enrolled in Brand Registry, and even non-luxury brands (Nike, Coach, etc.) are increasingly protecting their categories.

Pro Tips for Navigating Gating in 2026

1. Verify before you source. Check category gating before placing your first order. It's far cheaper to verify for 5 minutes than to order 500 units of ungatable products.

2. Request approval early. If you want to sell in gated categories, request approval 2-4 weeks before you plan to launch. Approval timelines are unpredictable, and delays can derail your launch timeline.

3. Use authorized distributors. When sourcing from wholesalers, specifically ask if they provide documentation for Amazon approval. Top distributors (Alibaba verified sellers, US-based wholesalers) typically include invoices automatically.

4. Stack multiple ungated categories. Rather than fighting for access to gated categories, build a diverse catalog across multiple ungated categories. This reduces risk and creates multiple revenue streams. Selecting the right products within those open categories is where solid Amazon product research becomes indispensable.

5. Monitor category status. Revisit category status every 3 months. Categories can shift status without warning, especially in response to brand requests or regulatory changes.

6. Automate approval tracking. If you're managing 10+ category approval requests, track them in a spreadsheet with approval dates, status, and required documentation. It prevents approval requests from falling through the cracks.

Start Selling on Amazon with Confidence

Understanding which categories are ungated is the first step to a successful Amazon business. By verifying category status, securing approvals early, and focusing on ungated categories with strong demand, you can launch profitable products without compliance headaches.
