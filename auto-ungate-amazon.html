How Auto-Ungate Works

Auto-Ungate is simple: open the tool, select the categories you want to apply for, and let it run. The extension cycles through your restricted items and submits applications automatically.

What Auto-Ungate Does, and What It Doesn't

We're honest about what this tool can and can't do. Read this carefully.

What It Does

  • Automates clicking the 'Apply' button for gated categories
  • Submits applications to multiple categories in bulk
  • Eliminates manual repetitive clicking
  • Works within Amazon's standard application process
  • Saves time vs. manual submissions
  • Included with all paid Ecom Circles plans

What It Doesn't

  • Guarantee approvals (Amazon decides, not us)
  • Improve your approval odds or chances
  • Bypass Amazon's gating restrictions
  • Influence Amazon's approval criteria
  • Skip the approval process
  • Work outside Amazon's approval system

How Approval Decisions Actually Work

Amazon approves or denies category ungating based on your account history, business performance, previous violations, inventory health, and current market conditions for that category. They do not care whether you submitted the application manually or through a tool. Auto-Ungate has zero effect on approval likelihood. We only save you time from repetitive clicking. The final approval decision is entirely Amazon's.

When Auto-Ungate Makes Sense
