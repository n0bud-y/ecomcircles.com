The 2026 Buy Box Algorithm: What Changed

In November 2025, Amazon fundamentally restructured how the Buy Box rotates. For years, the algorithm was straightforward: lowest price wins. But that created race-to-the-bottom dynamics that hurt sellers and customer experience. The new algorithm is more balanced, and much more favorable to FBM sellers with fast shipping.

Delivery Speed: 25-30% Weight

Sellers shipping within 1-2 days now compete head-to-head with FBA. If your delivery is as fast as FBA, the algorithm rotates you into the Buy Box rotation even if you're slightly higher priced.

Price: 20-25% Weight (Down from 50%)

Price still matters, but it's no longer the deciding factor. You don't need to match the lowest price to win rotations. Being within a "fairness threshold" (typically 5-15% above lowest) keeps you competitive.

Seller Metrics & Performance: 15-20% Weight

Feedback rating, defect rate, late shipment rate, and return rate now heavily influence rotation. A seller with 4.8+ stars and clean metrics wins rotations over cheaper competitors.

The "Fairness Threshold"

Amazon penalizes extreme undercutting. If the lowest-priced offer is $20 and you're at $35, the algorithm may skip you for Buy Box rotation. But if you're at $20.50-$22.50, you stay competitive. Pure price-matching repricing is dead.

What This Means for Repricing: Your repricer must be smart. It needs to factor in your fulfillment method, delivery speed, seller metrics, and market conditions. Blindly matching the lowest price guarantees margin erosion. Our repricer optimizes repricing for the post-November 2025 algorithm, helping you win Buy Box rotations without sacrificing profit.
