How Bulk Delete Works

The bulk delete tool integrates into your Walmart seller dashboard. Select the listings you want to remove, optionally filter by category or price, review your selection, and confirm deletion. All in one place.

1

Log Into Walmart Seller Central

Access your Walmart seller account as usual. The bulk delete tool appears in your Listings tab.

2

Select Listings to Delete

Check the boxes next to the listings you want to remove. You can select a few or dozens at once.

3

Apply Filters (Optional)

Filter by category, price range, or status to narrow your selection. Skip this step to delete everything you selected.

4

Review & Confirm

Review the listings you're about to delete. Once you confirm, the deletion is permanent.
