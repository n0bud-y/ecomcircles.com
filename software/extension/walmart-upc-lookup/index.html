Walmart UPC Requirements vs Amazon UPC Requirements

Walmart requires valid UPC codes on nearly all products. Unlike Amazon, Walmart does not allow variation within UPC codes for different variants (size, color); each variant must have its own unique UPC. Walmart's UPC requirements are stricter than Amazon's, making accurate barcode verification critical before listing.

Understanding Walmart's specific UPC rules is critical for successful selling.

What Walmart Requires

  • Valid, active UPC code for nearly every product
  • Unique UPC per variant (different sizes/colors = different UPCs)
  • UPC must be registered with GS1 (Global Standards Organization)
  • UPC cannot be duplicated across Walmart (no sharing codes)
  • Certain categories (grocery, health, beauty) require UPC before approval

Walmart vs Amazon: Key Differences

  • Amazon allows seller-generated UPCs under certain conditions; Walmart does not
  • Amazon allows ASIN-to-UPC mapping; Walmart requires strict UPC uniqueness
  • Walmart’s category restrictions are more stringent (grocery approval is harder to get)
  • Walmart verifies UPC registration with GS1; Amazon does not always verify
  • Missing UPC on Walmart = automatic rejection; Amazon may approve without one

  • Check for duplicate UPC issues in your inventory
  • Ensure variant products have unique UPCs
  • Identify category restrictions tied to UPC verification
  • Bulk-check all your products before upload
